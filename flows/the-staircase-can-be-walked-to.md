# The staircase can be walked to

**Covers:** acceptance criterion 10.

## Objective

Open the home-design studio at {{APP_URL}}.

The flight of stairs must have a door leading into it, and the foot of it must be walkable to from the front door. Do exactly this:

1. In the **Project navigator** on the left, click the floor row named **Ground floor**.
2. Find the staircase in the drawing: a flight of parallel tread lines with an arrow showing the way it rises.
3. Assert that a door is drawn into the staircase, that is, an opening in a wall of the stair well with a swing arc.
4. Click the **Checks** tab in the panel on the right and record any finding about the staircase, in particular one that says the staircase has no door or cannot be walked to.
5. In the Project navigator, click the floor row named **First floor**, and confirm the staircase is drawn there too, as the flight arriving at that level. The two storeys are never shown together — look at one, note it, then switch to the other. Do not wait for a combined view; there is not one.

Report whether the staircase has a door into it, what the Checks tab says about it, and whether it is drawn on both storeys. Finish with a plain statement of whether the stair is usable. Do not wait for anything else before reporting.
