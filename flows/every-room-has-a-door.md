# Every room has a door

**Covers:** acceptance criterion 7.

## Objective

Open the home-design studio at {{APP_URL}}.

Every room on the plan must have at least one door. Do exactly this:

1. Click the **Checks** tab in the panel on the right and read the list of findings it shows.
2. Look for any finding whose text says a room has no door, and record the room name it gives.
3. In the **Project navigator** on the left, click the floor row named **First floor**, read the Checks tab again, and record any finding that says a room has no door on that storey.
4. Read the status bar along the bottom of the screen and record the numbers next to Net indoor, Coverage and FAR, and whether the chip there says No errors.
5. Assert that no finding anywhere says a room has no door.

Report every room named in such a finding, or state plainly that there were none. Finish with the numbers from the status bar and the result of the assertion.
