# Nothing stands in a doorway

**Covers:** acceptance criterion 11.

## Objective

Open the home-design studio at {{APP_URL}}.

No piece of furniture may stand in the floor in front of a door. Do exactly this:

1. Click the **Checks** tab in the panel on the right and record every finding that says a piece of furniture blocks a doorway, with the piece of furniture it names and the room it is in.
2. In the **Project navigator** on the left, click the floor row named **First floor**, read the Checks tab again, and record any such finding on that storey.
3. In the drawing, find one door and look at the floor immediately in front of it, on the room side. Confirm the furniture rectangles are clear of that floor.
4. Assert there is no finding anywhere that says a piece of furniture blocks a doorway.

Report every piece of furniture that blocks a doorway, or state plainly that there were none. Finish with the result of the assertion.
