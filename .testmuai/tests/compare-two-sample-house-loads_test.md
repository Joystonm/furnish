# Compare two sample-house loads

> Load the sample twice and compare its rooms, their locations, and their areas across the two drawings.

## Step 1

At {{start_url}}, view the Furnish landing page and record the house plan shown there as the visual reference.

## Step 2

From the Furnish landing page, open the studio brief screen.

## Step 3

On the studio brief screen, use Load the sample plan to display the sample house.

## Step 4 @verifies ac-1, ac-2, ac-3, ac-4, ac-5, ac-6, ac-7, ac-8

On the loaded sample drawing, inspect the depicted house against the landing-page reference and its provenance statement, then assert that it matches the landing-page house, states that no model planned it, and visibly contains rooms, walls, doors with swings, windows, a staircase and furniture.

## Step 5

On the first loaded sample drawing, record each room's identity, location within the plan and displayed area for comparison with the next load.

## Step 6 @verifies ac-10, ac-11, ac-12

Return to the studio brief screen and use Load the sample plan again, then assert that the second plan has exactly the same room identities, each room occupies the same location, and each room shows the same area as on the first load.
